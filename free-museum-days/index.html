Free Museum Days in Toronto for Kids: The Always-Current Guide

By Joshua Opolko · Updated November 09, 2026 · Data refreshed daily at 6 AM ET

Toronto's major museums are free far more often than most families realize — the ROM is free one evening a month, the AGO another, and the City's ten history museums have free general admission. ROM: Third Tuesday Nights Free

The Royal Ontario Museum opens free of charge on the third Tuesday evening of every month, 4:00–8:30 PM. Advance tickets are required and walk-ins are not accepted, so book as soon as slots open. Upcoming dates: October 20, November 17 and December 15 (tickets are released about two weeks ahead and can sell out). Evening timing suits school-age kids better than toddlers.

AGO: First Wednesday Nights

The Art Gallery of Ontario is free on the first Wednesday of each month from 6 to 9 PM. Tickets must be booked online; they are released the Monday before each session and limited per booking, and they go quickly. Separately, the AGO lists free admission for Ontarians under 25 year-round (ID required for ages 18 to 24).

Always free: Toronto History Museums

The City of Toronto's ten history museums — including Fort York National Historic Site, Spadina Museum, Mackenzie House, Colborne Lodge in High Park, Montgomery's Inn, and Scarborough Museum — offer free general admission. Several run regular family programming and PA-day activities that appear on our tracker.

Make it a habit

Free nights are popular and ticket windows are short. The reliable pattern: AGO tickets on the Monday before the first Wednesday, ROM tickets as soon as the third-Tuesday release opens, and the daily tracker for everything in between — museum free days are tagged under the Entertainment and Learning categories.

Frequently asked questions

When is the ROM free in 2026?

The Royal Ontario Museum is free on the third Tuesday evening of every month from 4:00 to 8:30 PM, with advance tickets required.

Is the AGO free for kids?

Yes — the Art Gallery of Ontario lists free admission for Ontarians under 25, so Ontario kids and teens get in free (ID required for ages 18 to 24). The gallery is also free for everyone on the first Wednesday evening of each month from 6 to 9 PM with advance online booking.

Which Toronto museums are always free?

The City of Toronto's ten history museums offer free general admission, including Fort York, Spadina Museum, Mackenzie House, Colborne Lodge, Montgomery's Inn and Scarborough Museum.

Do free museum nights require tickets?

Usually yes. The ROM's free third-Tuesday nights require advance tickets and accept no walk-ins, and AGO first-Wednesday tickets are released online ahead of time, with a limited number per booking.
