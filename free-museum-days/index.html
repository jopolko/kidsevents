Free Museum Days in Toronto for Kids: The Always-Current Guide

By Joshua Opolko · Updated July 09, 2026 · Data refreshed daily at 6 AM ET

ROM: Third Tuesday Nights Free

The Royal Ontario Museum opens free of charge on the third Tuesday evening of every month, 4:00–8:30 PM. Advance tickets are required and walk-ins are not accepted, so book as soon as slots open. Remaining 2026 dates: June 16, July 21, August 18, September 15, October 20, November 17, and December 15. Evening timing suits school-age kids better than toddlers.

AGO: First Wednesday Nights

The Art Gallery of Ontario is free on the first Wednesday of each month from 6 to 9 PM. Tickets must be booked online; they are released the Monday before each session and capped at four per booking, and they go quickly. Separately, the AGO's free Annual Pass gives visitors aged 25 and under free admission year-round — which covers every kid and teen, any day the gallery is open.

Summer 2026: the Canada Strong Pass

From June 19 to September 7, 2026, the federal Canada Strong Pass program makes national museums free or reduced for young visitors. In Toronto that includes free ROM general admission for ages 4–17 and free Ontario Science Centre admission for ages 17 and under. No physical pass is needed — the discount applies at participating institutions.

Always free: Toronto History Museums

The City of Toronto's ten history museums — including Fort York National Historic Site, Spadina Museum, Mackenzie House, Colborne Lodge in High Park, Montgomery's Inn, and Scarborough Museum — offer free general admission. Several run regular family programming and PA-day activities that appear on our tracker.

Make it a habit

Free nights are popular and ticket windows are short. The reliable pattern: AGO tickets on the Monday before the first Wednesday, ROM tickets as soon as the third-Tuesday release opens, and the daily tracker for everything in between — museum free days are tagged under the Entertainment and Learning categories.

Frequently asked questions

When is the ROM free in 2026?

The Royal Ontario Museum is free on the third Tuesday evening of every month from 4:00 to 8:30 PM, with advance tickets required. From June 19 to September 7, 2026, general admission is also free for visitors aged 4 to 17 through the Canada Strong Pass program.

Is the AGO free for kids?

Yes — the Art Gallery of Ontario offers a free Annual Pass for anyone 25 and under, which means kids and teens get in free every day. The gallery is also free for everyone on the first Wednesday evening of each month from 6 to 9 PM with advance online booking.

Which Toronto museums are always free?

The City of Toronto's ten history museums offer free general admission, including Fort York, Spadina Museum, Mackenzie House, Colborne Lodge, Montgomery's Inn and Scarborough Museum.

Do free museum nights require tickets?

Usually yes. The ROM's free third-Tuesday nights require advance tickets and accept no walk-ins, and AGO first-Wednesday tickets are released online the Monday before, capped at four per booking.
